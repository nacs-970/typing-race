<objective>
Restore the multi-tab session-takeover notify-then-evict behavior that was a
working Phase 4 deliverable (`04-02-PLAN.md`) and was silently dropped during
the Phase 7 monolith-to-N-tier split. `RoomManager.rejoinPlayer()` currently
reassigns `room.players` from `oldPlayerId` to `newPlayerId` on a multi-tab
rejoin but never notifies or evicts the superseded socket, so the stale tab
sits connected, believing it still owns the player slot.

Purpose: Restore requirement REQ-07 ("Reconnect mid-race without corrupting
race state") to fully-satisfied — this is the multi-tab half of that
requirement, found broken by `/gsd-audit-milestone`
(`.planning/v1.0-MILESTONE-AUDIT.md`, finding REQ-07-multitab). Root cause,
exact fix location, and exact fix mechanism are already fully diagnosed in
`07.1-CONTEXT.md` (D-01 through D-04) — this plan translates those decisions
directly into code, no new design work.

Output: `rejoinPlayer()` publishes the two missing bridge events on a
genuine multi-tab takeover; a regression test locks in both the fixed path
and the still-correct duplicate/retried-rejoin-on-the-same-connection path.
</objective>
