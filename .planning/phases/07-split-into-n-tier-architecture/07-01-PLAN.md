<threat_model>
ASVS Level 1 Threat Model:
1. Threat: Memory exhaustion or orphaned room leaks in Engine if disconnect grace fails or client sockets drop ungracefully.
   - Severity: High (Mitigated).
   - Mitigation: Engine 1Hz tick loop monitors `player.disconnectedAt`. Once elapsed time exceeds 60,000ms, the player is evicted, host promotion is evaluated, and empty rooms are pruned from `RoomStore`.
2. Threat: Cheating or spoofed timestamps injected via the inter-service bridge.
   - Severity: High (Mitigated).
   - Mitigation: `GatewayToEngineEvent` attaches authoritative `serverTs` at the Gateway ingress layer. Keystroke anti-cheat validation in `apps/engine` continues to evaluate against server timestamp and 20ms minimum interval bounds.
3. Threat: Unauthenticated or malformed IPC messages crashing the Engine loopback server.
   - Severity: Medium (Mitigated).
   - Mitigation: In split mode, LoopbackIpcServer listens solely on localhost (`127.0.0.1:ENGINE_PORT`), rejecting remote connections. Inbound JSON frames are strictly parsed and validated against `GatewayToEngineEvent` schemas before dispatch.
</threat_model>

</objective>
